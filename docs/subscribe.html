DC Council Weekly Digest

A sourced weekly email that turns DC Council hearings, oversight documents, legislation, and local coverage into a readable briefing shaped around the issues you care about.

Follow local government without reading every agenda.

This project is developed by Prof. Michael A. Bailey and his team at McCourt School of Public Policy, Georgetown University. The goal is to make local government activity easier to follow by automatically collecting DC Council hearings, agendas, oversight documents, and related news, then summarizing the most important developments each week.

We want this digest to be accurate, readable, and useful for residents, advocates, and policymakers. Suggestions and feedback are welcome at gz163@georgetown.edu.
